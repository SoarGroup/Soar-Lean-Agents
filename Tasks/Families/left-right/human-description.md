# Left or right

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

You may move either left or right, once. Moving right earns a reward of +1 and moving left a reward of -1, but you are
not told this in advance: over repeated tries, learn to choose the better direction.

## Note

This is the reinforcement-learning example of the Soar Tutorial, not a task given to people.
