## Assumption ledger

- **A-WJ1** (statements 1): Two jug objects, three-gallon and five-gallon, with a capacity and the amount of water in them (both 0 at the start). The supply of water is not an object: fill needs nothing.
- **A-WJ2** (statements 2, 3): Pouring needs the amount that fits in the receiving jug, room = capacity - amount, in a condition, where arithmetic cannot be compiled; so each jug keeps room as a property beside its amount, and every operator updates both (with minus, added to WorldSpec 26 Nov 2026; the first version kept negated copies of the amounts, since WorldSpec then had only addition).
- **A-WJ3** (statements 3): "until the receiving jug is full or the pouring jug is empty": two operators. pour-all when the pouring jug's amount fits in the room of the other (the pouring jug ends empty); pour-until-full when it is more than the room (the receiving jug ends full and the rest stays behind).
- **A-WJ4** (statements 2): Filling a full jug or emptying an empty one changes nothing, so fill needs room in the jug and empty needs water in it.
- **A-WJ5** (statements none): No repeated-situation control: it compares relations, and the amounts are numbers on the jugs. The look-ahead's depth limit bounds the search.
