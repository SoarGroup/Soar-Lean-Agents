Sorting with phases: English input (Rosie teaching script)

The instructor's statements to Rosie, verbatim and in order (script.txt). "Load <name>" sets up the world state in <name>.world in this folder. A bare "Ok." from the instructor answers Rosie's request to set up a state (the puzzle, a failure condition or the goal), "Done." ends the teaching, and the final "No." declines Rosie's offer to solve the task. Rosie's side of the dialog is in dialog.txt; dialog.html shows the dialog with pictures of the world states.

Teaching script:
  1. The name of the puzzle is sorting-puzzle.
  2. Load init-sorting.   [world state: init-sorting.world]
  3. Ok.
  4. You can move a block onto a location.
  5. Load final-sorting.   [world state: final-sorting.world]
  6. The goal is that all the red blocks are on a red location and all the blue blocks are on a blue location and all the green blocks are on a green location.
  7. Ok.
  8. Done.
  9. No.

World vocabulary (extracted from the world files loaded above; not part of the dialog):
  Object categories: block, location
  Object attributes: color (blue1, green1, red1)
  Relations: below1, on1
  Names are as written in the world files, including their trailing 1 (red1, on1).

Source: IJCAI 2019 teaching data for games and puzzles, teaching-data/block-puzzles/sorting/.

Phases and loops (added 24 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird; a test of World-Conventions L8-026 to L8-030, not part of the Rosie teaching data):
Goal gathered: all the blocks are on the red location.
Phases: first goal, then gathered.
Repeat: from goal, 2 times.
