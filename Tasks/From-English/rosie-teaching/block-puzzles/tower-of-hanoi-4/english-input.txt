Tower of Hanoi (4 blocks): English input (Rosie teaching script)

The instructor's statements to Rosie, verbatim and in order (script.txt). "Load <name>" sets up the world state in <name>.world in this folder. A bare "Ok." from the instructor answers Rosie's request to set up a state (the puzzle, a failure condition or the goal), "Done." ends the teaching, and the final "No." declines Rosie's offer to solve the task. Rosie's side of the dialog is in dialog.txt; dialog.html shows the dialog with pictures of the world states.

Teaching script:
  1. The name of the puzzle is tower-of-hanoi-4.
  2. Load init-tower4.   [world state: init-tower4.world]
  3. Ok.
  4. You can move a clear block onto a clear object that is larger than the block.
  5. If a location is not below an object then it is clear.
  6. If the volume of a block is more than the volume of an object then the block is larger than the object.
  7. Load final-tower4.   [world state: final-tower4.world]
  8. The goal is that there are two clear green locations.
  9. Ok.
 10. Done.
 11. No.

World vocabulary (extracted from the world files loaded above; not part of the dialog):
  Object categories: block, location
  Object attributes: color (blue1, green1); size (large1, medium1, small1, tiny1); volume (numeric, 1 to 5)
  Relations: below1, on1
  Names are as written in the world files, including their trailing 1 (red1, on1).

Source: IJCAI 2019 teaching data for games and puzzles, teaching-data/block-puzzles/tower-of-hanoi-4/.

Search control (added 22 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird; not part of the Rosie teaching data; it augments the puzzle and does not change it. Select items with tools/control.py, e.g. python3 gen.py --control SC-1):
  SC-1 [operator-preference]: During look-ahead, do not move the smallest block twice in a row.
  SC-2 [operator-preference]: Work first on the largest block that is not yet in place: never move a block that is in place, and move the largest block that is not in place onto its place whenever it can. A block is in place when it is the largest block and is on the blue location, or when it is on the next larger block and that block is in place.
  SC-3 [operator-preference]: In look-ahead, do not move the smallest block back to the location it was on before (its peg: the location it is on directly or through the blocks below it).
