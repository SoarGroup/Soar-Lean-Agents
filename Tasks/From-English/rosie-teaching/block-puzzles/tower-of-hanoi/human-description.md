# Tower of Hanoi (3 disks)

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Three pegs, A, B and C, and a stack of disks of different sizes, all on peg A, largest at the bottom. Move one disk at a
time, taking only the top disk of a peg and placing it on an empty peg or on a larger disk; a disk may never rest on a
smaller one.

Goal: move the whole stack to another peg.

## This instance

Three disks. In the Rosie version the pegs are locations, the goal is that two locations marked green are empty, i.e. the whole stack has moved to the third location (init-tower3.world, final-tower3.world).
