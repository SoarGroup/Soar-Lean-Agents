# Eight puzzle (second instance)

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

A 3 x 3 tray holds eight numbered tiles and one empty square. A tile next to the empty square (left, right, above or
below, not diagonally) may slide into it, leaving its own square empty. Tiles may not be lifted out.

Goal: slide tiles until they match the target arrangement.
