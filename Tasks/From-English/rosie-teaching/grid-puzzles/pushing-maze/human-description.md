# Pushing maze

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Move your piece through a maze one square at a time. Where a box blocks the way you may push it one square ahead if
that square is empty, as in Sokoban.

Goal: reach the marked square.

## This instance

Your piece is the blue block; the goal is the blue location (init-pushmaze.world).

## Note

A small maze variant from the Rosie archive; the push rule is Sokoban's.
