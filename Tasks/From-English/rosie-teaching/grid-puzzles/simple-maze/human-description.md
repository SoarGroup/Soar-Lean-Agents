# Maze

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Move your piece through a maze one square at a time, up, down, left or right, onto empty squares only.

Goal: reach the marked square.

## This instance

Your piece is the blue block (init-mazerc.world, final-mazerc.world).

## Note

A plain maze from the Rosie archive; it has no standard published statement.
