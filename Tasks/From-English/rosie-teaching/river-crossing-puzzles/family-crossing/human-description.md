# Family crossing

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

A father, a mother and two children must cross a river in a small boat that holds either one adult or two children (or
one child). Anyone may row, and the boat must have someone in it to cross.

Goal: get the whole family across.

## This instance

Start: init-familycross.world; the solution takes nine crossings.
