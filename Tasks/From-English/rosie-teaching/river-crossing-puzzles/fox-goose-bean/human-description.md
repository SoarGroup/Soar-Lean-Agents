# Fox, goose and beans

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

A farmer must take a fox, a goose and a bag of beans across a river. His boat carries him and at most one of the three.
If left together without the farmer, the fox eats the goose, and the goose eats the beans.

Goal: get the farmer and all three across.
