# Jealous husbands

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Three married couples must cross a river in a boat that holds at most two people. No woman may be with another man
unless her husband is also present.

Goal: get all six across.
