Jealous Wives: English input (Rosie teaching script)

The instructor's statements to Rosie, verbatim and in order (script.txt). "Load <name>" sets up the world state in <name>.world in this folder. A bare "Ok." from the instructor answers Rosie's request to set up a state (the puzzle, a failure condition or the goal), "Done." ends the teaching, and the final "No." declines Rosie's offer to solve the task. Rosie's side of the dialog is in dialog.txt; dialog.html shows the dialog with pictures of the world states.

Teaching script:
  1. The name of the puzzle is jealous-wives.
  2. Load init-husbands.   [world state: init-husbands.world]
  3. Ok.
  4. The name of an action is ferry-one.
  5. You can move the boat and a block on the current bank onto the opposite bank.
  6. If an object is below a boat then it is current.
  7. If a location is not below a boat then it is opposite.
  8. The name of an action is ferry-two.
  9. You can move a block that is on the current bank and another block that is on the current bank and the boat onto the opposite bank.
 10. The solution has five steps.
 11. Load fail-wives.   [world state: fail-wives.world]
 12. If a man is on a bank and the wife of the man is not on the bank and another woman is on the bank then you lose.
 13. Ok.
 14. If the last-name of a man is the last-name of a woman then the woman is the wife of the man.
 15. Load final-husbands.   [world state: final-husbands.world]
 16. The name of the goal is all-crossed.
 17. Ok.
 18. The goal is that all the blocks are on a destination bank.
 19. Done.
 20. No.

World vocabulary (extracted from the world files loaded above; not part of the dialog):
  Object categories: block, location
  Object attributes: last-name (bale1, rogers1, smith1); name (destination1); shape (bank1, boat1, man1, woman1)
  Relations: below1, on1
  Names are as written in the world files, including their trailing 1 (red1, on1).

Other world files in this folder, not loaded in the dialog: init-husbands2.world
