# Four knights (Guarini's puzzle)

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

On a 3 x 3 board two white knights occupy two corners and two black knights the other two. Move them with knight moves,
one at a time, only onto empty squares.

Goal: exchange the white and black knights.

Board and knights: init-kswap2.world.
