# Eight men on a raft

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

The frogs-and-toads puzzle with four pieces on each side: four red and four blue pieces on a row of nine places with the
middle place empty. Blue pieces move only right and red pieces only left, by sliding into the next empty place or
jumping over one piece into the empty place beyond.

Goal: swap the reds and the blues.

## This instance

Nine places (init-8men.world); the solution takes 24 moves.

## Note

Eight men on a raft is the Rosie name for the four-a-side frogs-and-toads puzzle.
