# Frogs and toads

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Toads sit on the left of a row of lily pads and frogs on the right, with one empty pad between them. Toads move only to
the right and frogs only to the left. A piece may slide onto the empty pad next to it, or jump over one piece of the
other kind onto the empty pad beyond.

Goal: swap the two groups.

## This instance

Two frogs and two toads on five pads (init-frog.world).
