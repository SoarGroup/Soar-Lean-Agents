# Knight swap

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

White and black knights stand at opposite sides of a small board. Move them with ordinary knight moves, one at a time,
only onto empty squares; the colours need not alternate.

Goal: exchange the positions of the white and black knights.

Board and knights: init-ksideswap2.world.
