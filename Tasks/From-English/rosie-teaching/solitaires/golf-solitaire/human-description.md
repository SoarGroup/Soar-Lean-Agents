# Golf solitaire

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Cards are dealt face up in columns, with one card on the foundation pile. You may move the exposed card of any column to
the foundation if its rank is one higher or one lower than the foundation's top card, regardless of suit; it then
becomes the new top card.

Goal: clear all the columns.

## This instance

A small deal of numbered cards (init-golf-sol.world); nine moves.
