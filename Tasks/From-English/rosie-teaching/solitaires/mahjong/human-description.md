# Mahjong solitaire

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Tiles are stacked in a layout. You may remove two identical tiles together if both are free: nothing lies on top of a
tile and it is open on its left or right side.

Goal: remove all the tiles.

## This instance

Tiles are matched by colour (init-mahjong.world).
