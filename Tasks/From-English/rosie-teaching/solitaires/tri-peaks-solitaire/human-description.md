# Tri Peaks solitaire

*Task description for a human solver, written 21 November 2026 by Claude, supervised by John E. Laird, from the public sources listed at the end (paraphrased, not quoted).*

Cards are laid out in three overlapping peaks. An uncovered card may go to the waste pile if its rank is one higher or one
lower than the waste pile's top card, regardless of suit; removing cards uncovers those beneath.

Goal: clear the peaks.

## This instance

A small layout of numbered cards (init-tri-peaks.world); nine moves.
