Mutilated checkerboard: English input

Statements, numbered (the input to formalization):
 1. A chessboard has 8 rows and 8 columns of squares, coloured alternately black and white.
 2. Two diagonally opposite corner squares are removed, leaving 62 squares.
 3. A domino covers exactly two squares that share a side.
 4. The question is whether 31 dominoes can cover the 62 remaining squares, with no overlap and nothing off the board.
 5. The goal is to answer the question and prove the answer.

Source of the task in AI research: McCarthy 1964 (representation-change challenge).
Kind of agent: Reasoning / proof.
Status: Asks for a proof of impossibility, not a plan. A Lean proof is natural; a Soar agent is not.
